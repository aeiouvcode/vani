Privacy

Speech models: Moonshine v2 (moonshine-ai, MIT). Denoise: GTCRN. VAD: Silero. All inference runs in this browser via WebAssembly. Recordings and corrections stay in local storage. Network is used only to download models from Hugging Face, once, then the app works offline.
